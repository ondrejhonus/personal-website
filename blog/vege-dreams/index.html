Vege Dreams: My Vegetarian Recipe Collection

📅 Published: Friday, January 23, 2026 📖 1 min read 📊 203 words 🏷️ Tags: recipes , cooking , webdev
This is a collection of my favorite vegetarian recipes that I have tried and loved over the years.

It would make me very happy, if you checked out my vegetarian recipe collection website called Vege Dreams.

What is Vege Dreams?

It’s a personal project of mine, where I share my favorite vegetarian recipes that I have tried and loved over the years. From Irish soda bread to Italian Spaghetti Carboanara (without bacon, of course!), there’s something for everyone.

What can you find there?

You can also find some vegan recipes there, and some recipes are very easy to adapt to be vegan-friendly, usually just remove cheese, or replace dairy with plant-based alternatives.

Where did the idea come from?

The website is actually a fork of my brothers website Tofu Dreams, which is focused on vegan recipes. I wanted to create something similar, but with a focus on vegetarian dishes, because I don’t think I’d be able to be a vegan, plus I had some of mine recipes in mind, so I really wanted to have something similar. https://github.com/dhonus/tofu-dreams

How is it built?

My version of the recipe collection is styled diffrently though, I am using Bulma.io CSS framework, which i really like. Feel free to check my code on Github, and maybe even fork it for your own recipe collection! https://github.com/)ondrejhonus/vege-dreams
