It would make me very happy, if you checked out my vegetarian recipe collection website called [Vege Dreams](https://veg.ondrejhonus.com/).

## What is Vege Dreams?

It's a personal project of mine, where I share my favorite vegetarian recipes that I have tried and loved over the years. From Irish soda bread to Italian Spaghetti Carboanara (without bacon, of course!), there's something for everyone.

## What can you find there?

You can also find some vegan recipes there, and some recipes are very easy to adapt to be vegan-friendly, usually just remove cheese, or replace dairy with plant-based alternatives.

## Where did the idea come from?

The website is actually a fork of my brothers website Tofu Dreams, which is focused on vegan recipes. I wanted to create something similar, but with a focus on vegetarian dishes, because I don't think I'd be able to be a vegan, plus I had some of mine recipes in mind, so I really wanted to have something similar.
https://github.com/dhonus/tofu-dreams

## How is it built?

My version of the recipe collection is styled diffrently though, I am using [Bulma.io](https://bulma.io/) CSS framework, which i really like.
