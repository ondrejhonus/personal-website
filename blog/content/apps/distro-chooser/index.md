## How does it work?

The tool asks you a series of questions about your preferences and needs, and based on your answers, it suggests 3 best Linux
distributions for you. The questions are very simple.

### Scoring System

Each distro has parameters like difficulty, stability, release cycle and gaming support, and based on your answers, the tool calculates the points for each distro and suggests the best ones for you.
